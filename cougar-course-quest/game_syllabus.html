Game Syllabus Guide

This is a short guide to the course topics used in the game. After an incorrect answer, Hint opens the relevant topic. The full official syllabus is in Blackboard Ultra and controls course policies and requirements.

Course access and weekly work

DDG 1100 is an asynchronous online course. A W section does not meet for scheduled online class sessions. Open the Blackboard modules each week for lectures, videos, tutorials, projects, and feedback. Use the Blackboard Course Calendar to confirm an assignment’s exact due date; regular asynchronous work is due Sunday at 11:59 p.m.

Use a computer that can run Adobe Creative Cloud and a reliable high-speed internet connection. Read the full syllabus for textbook and software requirements. Adobe Creative Cloud access may pause between semesters. If Blackboard has a problem near a deadline, contact your instructor promptly and document what happened.

Contacting your professor

Use your Columbus State CougarMail account in Outlook for course email. Personal email accounts may not receive a response. Include a subject line, your class name, and its five-digit course number so your professor can identify the course and the reason for your message.

Do not assume that email will be answered over a weekend. Consult the full course syllabus in Blackboard Ultra for your instructor’s hours and response expectations.

Projects and deadlines

Submit projects through the appropriate Blackboard assignment link. Email and social media are not project submission methods. Include the requested native project files and original image files with embedded metadata when an assignment requires them.

Late work in this DDG 1100 syllabus has a 5% deduction for each day late and is not accepted more than seven days after its deadline. The final assignment has a separate no-late-submission rule. Read the full course syllabus and each project brief for the instructions that apply.

Participation and student support

Participation means significant academic activity in the course, such as completing coursework. Logging in alone does not demonstrate participation. Columbus State verifies engagement for federal aid reporting, and students who do not attend or participate may face administrative withdrawal.

Questions about financial aid, enrollment, or how a withdrawal affects aid belong with the Financial Aid Office. Review the college information and your full syllabus in Blackboard Ultra.

Original work and AI

Complete and submit your own original design work. Submitting someone else’s work or screenshots of online images as your own may be treated as academic misconduct. Missing native files or metadata can affect whether a project is accepted or graded. Plagiarism can occur whether the act was intentional or unintentional.

DDG 1100 allows only the AI tools identified in its full syllabus, including the tools offered within the Adobe programs specified there. Using outside AI tools for a project may result in a failing project grade. Check the official course syllabus before using a tool; rules may differ in another class.

Learning goals and assessment

DDG 1100 includes Critical Thinking among its Institutional Learning Goals. Student work is assessed against course outcomes, and outcome results are reported without student names to help improve instruction.
